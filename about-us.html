About GetAllTix.com

One search. Every marketplace. All-in pricing.

Our mission

GetAllTix.com was built for fans who are tired of opening a dozen browser tabs to figure out which site has the best seats at the best price. We aggregate live inventory from the major secondary marketplaces, official team and venue sellers, and trusted resale partners — then show every option side-by-side with transparent, all-in pricing.

What we do

We index millions of listings across sports, concerts, theater, and live events. When you search for a team, artist, venue, or event, we surface the lowest verified price for each seat — including fees — and link you directly to the marketplace selling the ticket. We never resell tickets ourselves; we simply make the market visible.

Why fans use us

Ticket prices for the same seat can vary by 30% or more between marketplaces, and headline prices rarely include fees. GetAllTix.com compares everything in real time so you always know you're getting a fair deal. No hidden markup. No upsells. Just the full picture.
